# Regional Journal of Teaching Practice: guidelines for reviewers

Reviewers judge each manuscript on these points:

1. **Relevance to practice.** The question matters to teachers, and the paper says what a teacher could do with the result.
2. **Fit between question and method.** The design can answer the question the paper asks.
3. **Claims supported by the data.** The conclusions do not go beyond what the results show.
4. **Limitations stated.** The paper names the limitations that matter for its conclusions.
5. **Clarity.** A teacher who is not a researcher can follow the paper.

Recommendations: accept, minor revision, major revision, reject.
