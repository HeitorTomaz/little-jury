# Plan: late fees

This change will put an end to late returns once and for all. It is the most elegant solution we found, and
readers will get it right away.

## What changes

1. Each day of late return generates a fee of $1.00 per book. (decided)
2. A reader with fees above $10.00 cannot borrow or renew until they pay. (decided)
3. The fee is paid at the desk, in cash; the librarian records the payment in the system. (decided)
4. The website shows the accumulated fee on the "My loans" page. (decided)
5. Students from the municipal school network may be exempt. (don't know yet)
6. Online payment through the website, in a second stage. (idea)

## Why

Last semester, 38% of loans were returned late (library report). The city hall asked for a measure.

## Constraints

- The change must go live at the start of the school semester, in February.
- We are two developers, part-time.
- Late returns from before the change do not generate fees.
