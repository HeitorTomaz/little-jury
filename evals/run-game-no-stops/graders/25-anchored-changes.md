In r4-debate.md, every final position on a debate point is classified as independent (held before any argument), verified (changed after an argument, quoting a passage of the description or a scenario; counted as one confirmation of that argument's origin) or persuaded (changed with no quote; not counted toward agreement); attributions in the moderator's summaries point to their source as (r<n>.md § <id>).
