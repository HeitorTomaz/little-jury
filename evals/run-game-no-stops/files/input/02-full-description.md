*Material from round 2 on. Based on version 0.1 of the concept document.*

**How to read:** each item carries a marker. **[decided]** is the author's choice. **[proposed]** is an idea not yet confirmed. **[undefined]** is a known gap. **[fact]** describes the author's situation. Evaluate what is written. If information is missing to judge something, point out the gap instead of assuming how it works.

None of these decisions is untouchable. The panel may recommend changing any of them.

## 1. Intended experience

- Winning comes from coordinating under pressure without words, not from reflexes. **[decided]**
- Both roles must feel essential in every match; neither player should become the other's assistant. **[decided]**
- The night should feel lonely and tense, with relief at dawn. **[decided]**
- Target audience. **[undefined]**

## 2. Structure of a match

- Two players, two fixed roles per match: the keeper (lamp room) and the runner (outside). Roles can be swapped between matches, not during one. **[decided]**
- A match is one night, split into six watches of about seven minutes each. **[decided]**
- The island has 8 locations connected by paths: lamp room, stairs, yard, supply shed, woodpile, jetty, cliff path and the old boathouse. **[decided]**
- Storm intensity, ship schedule and breakdowns are drawn at the start of the match from a seed shown on screen, so a night can be replayed. **[decided]**

## 3. The keeper

- Keeps the lamp lit by spending oil; one unit of oil lasts one watch. **[decided]**
- Cleans the lens; a dirty lens halves the light's reach and makes ships less likely to see it. **[decided]**
- Fixes the clockwork that turns the lamp; when it jams, the light stops turning and counts as off for passing ships. **[decided]**
- Cannot leave the lamp room while the lamp is lit. **[decided]**
- Can see the whole island from the window, but only in flashes, when the beam sweeps across it. **[proposed]**

## 4. The runner

- Carries at most two items at a time (oil, driftwood, tools). **[decided]**
- Repairs shutters, the stove, the yard bell and the supply shed door. **[decided]**
- Gets colder outside; at full cold they must return to the stove or collapse and lose the rest of the watch. **[decided]**
- Can climb to the lamp room to hand over oil; the stairs take a third of a watch each way. **[decided]**

## 5. Signals

- The players cannot type or speak inside the game. **[decided]**
- The keeper signals with short and long flashes; the runner signals with the yard bell (one, two or three rings). **[decided]**
- There is no fixed code: players agree on meanings among themselves. **[decided]**
- An optional in-game voice chat for online matches. **[proposed]**

## 6. Ships, losing and winning

- Ships pass at times drawn from the seed; each ship needs the light on and turning when it passes. **[decided]**
- Each lost ship makes the storm one step stronger for the rest of the night. **[decided]**
- Reaching dawn with at least 5 of 8 ships saved is a win. **[decided]**
- If the lamp goes out completely during a storm peak, the match ends immediately. **[decided]**
- A complete lamp failure costs one of three "lamp lives"; the match ends when the third is lost. **[decided]**
- What happens at the end of a lost match (summary, replay, progression). **[undefined]**

## 7. Breakdowns

- Each watch draws one to three breakdowns from a table of twelve. **[decided]**
- Some breakdowns can only be fixed by the runner, some only by the keeper, and two need both players at the same place. **[decided]**
- A breakdown left unfixed for a full watch becomes permanent for the rest of the night. **[proposed]**

## 8. Progression

- There is no progression between nights in the first version: every night starts from zero. **[decided for the first version]**
- Unlockable islands with different layouts. **[proposed]**
- How difficulty rises between nights. **[undefined]**

## 9. Long-term goal

- Online matches between two computers. **[decided as a future goal]**
- How the signals and the no-talking rule would work online, where players could use an outside voice app. **[undefined]**

## 10. Launch and technology

- The first version will be sold on a PC game store at a single price, with no in-game purchases. **[decided]**
- Which store and what price. **[undefined; the author asks the panel for a recommendation]**
- Built in the Godot engine. **[decided]**
- The author is a team of two people working part-time; one programs, one does art and sound. **[fact]**
- The author has never published a game on a PC store. **[fact]**
- Tests will be done with friends playing a rough prototype on the same couch; there will be no paid playtests. **[decided]**
