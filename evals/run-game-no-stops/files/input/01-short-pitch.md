# Short pitch

**Name:** not defined yet

A cooperative game for two people who keep a lighthouse running through one stormy night.

Each match takes place on a small rocky island. One player stays in the lamp room and keeps the light turning: feeding it oil, cleaning the lens, fixing the clockwork when it jams. The other goes out into the weather to gather oil from the supply shed, repair the shutters and haul driftwood to keep the stove alive. Neither can do the whole job alone, and the two jobs pull in different directions: the lamp needs constant care, and the island keeps breaking.

Ships pass during the night. If the light is on when a ship passes, the ship is safe; if it is off, the ship runs aground and the night gets harder. You win if you reach dawn with enough ships saved.

The players cannot talk inside the game. They signal to each other with the lamp itself (short and long flashes) and with a bell in the yard. Players sitting on the same couch can talk out loud, of course; the game is designed to work without that.

Every night is different: the storm, the ships' schedule and what breaks are drawn at the start of the match. A match is meant to last about forty minutes.

The first version is for two players on the same computer. The intention, later, is to allow online matches between two computers.

There are no enemies and no combat. The pressure comes from the storm, the clock and the other player being out of reach.
