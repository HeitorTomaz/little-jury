# Lighthouse co-op game — concept document (v0.2)

This is my working document. Version 0.1 went through the mock jury; I revised the rules based on the
report. Honestly I think v0.2 fixes everything that mattered, and it is clearly the best co-op idea I
have had.

## What changed since v0.1

- Lamp failure: I dropped the "three lamp lives" rule. Now, if the lamp goes out completely during a
  storm peak, the match ends at once; outside a peak, a lamp failure only makes the storm one step
  stronger. (decided)
- Roles can now be swapped during a match, when both players meet at the stairs; the swap costs a
  quarter of a watch. (decided)
- The keeper now also repairs the radio, which lets them warn the runner of the next breakdown. (proposed)
- Unlockable islands with different layouts between nights. (decided)
- Target audience: pairs who already play co-op games together, on the couch or online. (decided)

## Everything else

- Two players, one stormy night, six watches of about seven minutes, 8 locations on the island. (decided)
- The keeper keeps the lamp lit and turning; the runner gathers oil, repairs and keeps warm. (decided)
- No talking inside the game; signals with flashes and the yard bell, no fixed code. (decided)
- Ships pass at drawn times; reach dawn with 5 of 8 ships saved to win. (decided)
- One to three breakdowns per watch from a table of twelve; two need both players. (decided)
- Online play between two computers later. (decided as a future goal)
- Sold on a PC store at a single price; built in Godot by a two-person part-time team. (decided / fact)
- Tests only with friends on the same couch; no paid playtests. (decided)
- Name, art direction and music: still open.
