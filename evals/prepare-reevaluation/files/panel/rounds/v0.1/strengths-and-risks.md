# Strengths and risks — v0.1

## Strengths
- Signals through the lamp and the bell (casual, coop-puzzlers, designer).

## Problems
1. **Two lamp-failure rules contradict each other** (proven problem): "the match ends immediately if the
   lamp goes out during a storm peak" versus "a complete lamp failure costs one of three lamp lives".
   Source: r2.md, systems and designer.
2. **The keeper's role gets boring** compared with the runner's (hypothesis): the keeper cannot leave
   the lamp room while it is lit. Source: r2.md, competitive and short-sessions; r3.md.
3. **One player can end up doing everything** (hypothesis): the runner carries the match while the keeper
   only refuels. Source: r3.md, systems and coop-puzzlers.
