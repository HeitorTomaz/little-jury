# Quiz 3: describing data (40 minutes)

Answer all four questions. Show your work for questions 3 and 4.

1. The heights, in centimetres, of seven plants are: 12, 15, 15, 18, 21, 22, 40. Find the median.

2. Which is larger for a data set, the mean or the median? Explain.

3. A class of 20 students has a mean test score of 70. A new student joins with a score of 91.
   What is the new mean?

4. A shop records the number of customers each hour: 3, 5, 5, 6, 8, 30. Which measure of centre best
   describes a typical hour? Justify your choice.
